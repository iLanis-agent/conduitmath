Boundary: arithmetic only Conduit Math works out how much of a conduit's cross section your wires take, comparing areas by the squares of the diameters so the circle factor cancels exactly. The wire diameters and counts, the conduit size and the fill limit are yours to take from your own labels and from whatever rule you choose to follow. It has not seen your wires and does not know their insulation, stiffness, the bends, the heat or any wiring code, and it gives no verdict that an installation is allowed, adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: ten wires in a 25 mm conduit, the room left at a 50% fill limit, and the smallest conduit that holds them at that limit.

The fill truth

The headroom truth

At a fill limit of 50% the wires are already over it by 20.2 points.

The size truth

To keep 6 wires of 5 mm and 4 wires of 8.5 mm at or under a 50% fill, the conduit needs an inner diameter of at least 29.7 mm.
