Conduit Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out how much of a conduit's cross section your wires take, comparing areas by the squares of the diameters so the circle factor cancels exactly. The wire diameters and counts, the conduit size and the fill limit are yours to take from your own labels and from whatever rule you choose to follow. It has not seen your wires and does not know their insulation, stiffness, the bends, the heat or any wiring code, and it gives no verdict that an installation is allowed, adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

The fill truth

Up to three kinds of wire, counted by you, with each outer diameter in mm, inside a conduit whose inner diameter you enter.

The headroom truth

How many more wires of kind 1 fit under the fill limit you chose, using the numbers above.

The size truth

The smallest conduit inner diameter, to a tenth of a mm, that keeps the same wires at or under the fill limit.

Conduit Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
